How the maths works

  1. Billable hours a year = billable hours a week × (52 − weeks off).
  2. Revenue you must invoice = (take-home target + fixed costs) ÷ (1 − tax rate).
  3. Real hourly rate = that revenue ÷ billable hours a year. Below it you are paying to work.
  4. Quote a little above it — the hint above adds a 15% safety margin for scope creep and unpaid invoices.
  5. Three packages use the same rate at 60% / 100% / 130% of the project hours, with a 15% priority premium on Premium.
